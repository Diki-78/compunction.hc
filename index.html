Compunction.hc

We know the weight of regret: the guilt, the shame, and the ache of letting down the people who matter to us. Sometimes we are not ready to face what we have done, but that does not make it any less ours to answer for. Through Compunction.hc, we turn that struggle into hardcore: owning our mistakes, carrying their consequences, and finding the courage to face them. Fight your regret, even when you are not ready.
