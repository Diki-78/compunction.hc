Compunction.hc

Compunction.hc was born from regret, guilt, and the weight of things left undone. Built from broken pieces, buried emotions, and the consequences of our own choices, we turn our inner conflicts into noise. Through crushing riffs, raw aggression, and relentless energy, we give a voice to the things we struggle to say. This is not about finding redemption or pretending everything is alright. It's about facing what remains, living with our scars, and letting the sound speak for itself. This is Compunction.HC. Born from remorse. Driven by noise.
